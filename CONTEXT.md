# Namaste Context

## Domain Glossary

- **App flow** — the local onboarding journey from onboarding to how it works, wallet top-up, and wallet home. Expo Router is still used for standalone routes such as scan QR.
- **Wallet home** — the post-onboarding wallet surface showing balance, wallet activity, saved QR places, profile summary, and top-level wallet actions.
- **Wallet top-up** — the flow where a traveler chooses a USD amount, sees the NPR quote, selects a payment method, and adds money to the wallet.
- **Scan QR session** — the active camera interaction for permission, torch state, QR detection, and scan result handling.
- **Wallet activity** — the transaction history shown on the wallet home surface.
- **Saved QR place** — a stored merchant or frequent payment destination shown from the wallet home surface.
- **Payment method** — the user-selected option for funding a wallet top-up.
